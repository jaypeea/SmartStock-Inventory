How Your App Prevents Excess Inventory & Stockouts

Every time you add a product or log a sale, JavaScript automatically runs these three supply-chain formulas:

01

Safety stock

(Peak daily sales × Longest lead time) − (Average daily sales × Average lead time)

Buffers demand spikes and supplier delays.

02

Reorder point

(Average daily sales × Average lead time) + Safety stock

Signals when to place the next order.

03

Stock ceiling

Reorder point + 7-day predicted demand

Units above this level are marked as excess.
